# Changelog

## 1.2.0

- In Simplified or Traditional Chinese, rows, actions, messages, and the
  detail pane are in Chinese too (3 段, 已拷贝 3 句). The generated text is
  unchanged.

## 1.1.0

- When Prism runs in Simplified or Traditional Chinese, it shows this
  extension's name, description, command, and setting in that language, and
  Chinese words find its command in search.

## 1.0.1

- Installs on Prism 0.1.0, which already pastes.

## 1.0.0

- Generate Lorem Ipsum: type how many paragraphs, sentences, or words, and
  paste or copy them, with a preview and word count in the detail pane.
- Placeholder text in Latin, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, German, or French,
  picked with ⌘P.
- Start with “Lorem ipsum”: begin Latin text with the classic opening, or not.
