# Lorem Ipsum

Generates placeholder text and pastes it, for mockups, layouts, and test data,
in Latin, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, German, or French.

**Generate Lorem Ipsum** lists paragraphs, sentences, and words. Type how
many, such as `3`, and narrow to one kind by adding its start: `5 w` for five
words, `20 sentences`, or `3 段` and `50字`. Up to 1,000 of each. ⌘P picks the
language, and the list opens in it next time. Return pastes the text into the
app you opened Prism over and leaves it on the clipboard; ⌘⇧C only copies it.
Each time you use the text, the next is new. ⌘R regenerates the list without
pasting. The detail pane shows the whole text with its word and character
counts.

Paragraphs have 4 to 8 sentences. Chinese and Japanese don't space their
words, so there a “word” is a character: `50字` is 50 characters. Every
language draws from a word list written for this extension, so the text means
nothing. With **Start with “Lorem ipsum”** on, Latin text opens with the
classic “Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit.”

## Setup

Pasting needs Accessibility access for Prism (System Settings → Privacy &
Security → Accessibility). Without it, the text is copied instead and Prism
says so.

Needs nothing else and declares no capabilities: the text is made on your Mac,
and nothing leaves it.
