# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Open Ports lists every process listening on a TCP port, with its ports as
  tags colored by whether each is open to this Mac only, to the network, or
  to one address.
- Kill, force kill, or kill the parent of the process on a port, after a
  confirmation; a process that ignores the request to quit is reported as
  still running.
- Open a port in your browser, show its program in Finder, or copy its PID,
  path, or command line, from menus in ⌘K.
- The detail lists every port, the owner, the parent, both programs’ paths,
  and when the process started.
- Name the ports you use often in Port Names.
