# Changelog

## 1.2.0

- In Simplified or Traditional Chinese, sections, actions, the access row,
  and messages are in Chinese too.

## 1.1.0

- When Prism runs in Simplified or Traditional Chinese, it shows this
  extension's name, description, command, and the request for access to the
  Notes folder in that language, and Chinese words find its command in
  search.

## 1.0.0

- Search Notes: search every note by title, content, folder, tags, or
  pinyin, title matches first, and open them in Notes.
- Tab on Notes' own row in root search opens Search Notes.
