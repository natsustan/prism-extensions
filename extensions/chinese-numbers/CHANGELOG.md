# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- When Prism runs in Simplified or Traditional Chinese, it shows this
  extension's name, description, and command in that language, and Chinese
  words find its command in search.

## 1.0.0

- Convert to Chinese Numbers: type digits to get them in Chinese lowercase,
  uppercase, and as an amount in words, or type a Chinese number to get its
  digits. Return pastes; ⌘⇧C copies.
