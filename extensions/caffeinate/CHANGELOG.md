# Changelog

## 1.3.0

- In Simplified or Traditional Chinese, the messages, the status row, and the
  widget are in Chinese too, coffee jokes included.

## 1.2.0

- When Prism runs in Simplified or Traditional Chinese, it shows this
  extension's commands, setting, and widget in that language (the name stays
  Caffeinate), and Chinese words such as 保持唤醒 find its commands in search.

## 1.1.0

- Runs as its own process, built with PrismExtensionKit, instead of inside
  Prism. Commands, the status row, the widget, and the preference are
  unchanged.
