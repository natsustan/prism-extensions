# Changelog

## 1.2.0

- In Simplified or Traditional Chinese, filters, categories, actions, setup
  rows, and messages are in Chinese too. Item and vault names stay as they
  are in 1Password.

## 1.1.0

- When Prism runs in Simplified or Traditional Chinese, it shows this
  extension's name, description, commands, and settings in that language,
  and Chinese words find its commands in search.

## 1.0.0

- Search 1Password: every account’s items, most recently changed first,
  with usernames, vaults, and favorites, narrowed by favorites, category, or
  vault with ⌘P.
- Paste or copy a login’s password, username, or one-time password, open
  its website, or open it in 1Password, with Raycast’s shortcuts. Passwords
  stay out of clipboard histories and are cleared after 90 seconds.
- Generate Password: random passwords and PINs to paste or copy.
- Signs in through the 1Password app as Raycast’s extension does: Sign In
  waits while 1Password asks you to allow Prism. Setup rows cover a missing
  CLI and the integration being off.
