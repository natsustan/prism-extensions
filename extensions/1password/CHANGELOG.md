# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Search 1Password: every account’s items, most recently changed first,
  with usernames, vaults, and favorites, narrowed by favorites, category, or
  vault with ⌘P.
- Paste or copy a login’s password, username, or one-time password, open
  its website, or open it in 1Password, with Raycast’s shortcuts. Passwords
  stay out of clipboard histories and are cleared after 90 seconds.
- Generate Password: random passwords and PINs to paste or copy.
- Signs in through the 1Password app as Raycast’s extension does, with a
  waiting row while 1Password asks you to allow Prism, and setup rows when
  the CLI is missing or its integration is off.
